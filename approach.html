APPROACH

Research is how I make the invisible visible.

I work across messy systems where the answer is not obvious, the stakeholders are many, and the experience is shaped by technology, policy, operations, space, and human behavior.

01 · Understand

I combine qualitative and quantitative methods to understand people in context: interviews, observation, journey mapping, surveys, benchmarking, document review, process analysis, simulation, and usability testing.

02 · Synthesize

I turn scattered evidence into patterns that teams can act on: personas, jobs-to-be-done, service blueprints, process maps, opportunity areas, decision criteria, and hypotheses.

03 · Decide

The strategic value often happens here. I facilitate teams through tradeoffs, prioritize opportunities by impact and effort, and make assumptions explicit so decisions can be tested rather than debated indefinitely.

04 · Design & test

When the work continues into design, I translate research into concepts, prototypes, service models, spatial programs, product requirements, and implementation roadmaps. When it does not, I make sure the research still produces a useful decision.

05 · Measure

I define success in terms that matter to the system: patient experience, adoption, operational efficiency, capacity, trust, cost, time, or other agreed outcomes. The metric depends on the problem—not the method.
