WHAT I DO

Research is only useful when it changes what happens next.

Research: ethnography, interviews, focus groups, usability testing, surveys, behavioral analysis, human factors, stakeholder research, systems mapping.

Strategy: journey maps, service blueprints, process maps, ecosystem maps, future-state vision, prioritization, product roadmaps, decision frameworks.

Design & implementation: prototyping, wireframing, pilot programs, UAT, change management, training, executive presentations, co-design.
